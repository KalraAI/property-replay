08 - Why it works

No footage required.

Buildings already narrate themselves - flow meters, alarms, HVAC, panel events. The claim file builds from data the property and the carrier already have; photos are supporting evidence, not the backbone.

The brain learns on synthetic losses.

Millions of simulated water, fire, storm and freeze events - every record labeled, every outcome graded. The generator writes the corpus; the grader is measured against it.

Signed records, not stories.

Every record is hashed, timestamped, and written to an audit ledger. The insurer gets a file it can defend, not a narrative it has to re-litigate.

09 - Where this sits

The same reconstruction engine that grades vehicle crashes grades property losses: telemetry and documents in, causality and liability graded, a signed claim-ready record out.
