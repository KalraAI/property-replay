09 - How it works

No video needed.

Buildings already record what happens: water flow, alarms, heating, electrical panel events. The replay is built from data the property and the insurer already have. Photos help, but they're not required.

It learns from synthetic losses.

Synthetic water, fire, storm and freeze losses, each with a known answer. We write the cases, then score the system against them.

Sealed records, not stories.

Every record is fingerprinted, time-stamped and written to an audit log. You get a record you can check, not a story you have to piece together again.

10 - What this is and isn't

Property Replay is in beta. It replays the data you give it and shows what's missing. A person makes the call. It does not decide cause, liability or coverage.
